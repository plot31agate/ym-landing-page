The basics

What is a prediction market?

YM Predictions is a leading prediction markets platform in Europe. So what's a prediction market? It's a peer-to-peer trading platform where you can trade on the outcome of real-life events. The best way to describe it is like a stock market, except instead of trading on the success of a company, you're trading on real-life questions. That could be anything from your favourite sport, to politics, to the outlandish: "Will aliens exist at the end of 2026?"

The mechanics

How do prediction markets work?

Everything effectively comes down to a Yes or No answer. If you think the answer is yes, you buy a Yes contract. If you think the answer is no, you buy a No contract. It's really that simple. The price is based on probability and shown as a percentage between 0% and 100%. So if something is sitting at 0.70, that indicates a 70% chance.

The great thing is, the odds aren't decided by a bookie or a casino. They're decided by the general consensus of everyone trading. It's also live, which means the price can move based on what the group is thinking at any given minute.

How it works

Prediction market examples.

What makes a prediction market different is that you aren't betting against YM Predictions. You're trading with others on a peer-to-peer platform. Three steps:

01

A market opens

For example: "Will Spain win Euro 2028?" YM Predictions then defines the resolution rules and the official source that will determine the outcome.

02

You trade on the outcome

Buy Yes or No depending on what you think will happen. You can also sell before resolution to lock in profits or cut losses.

03

The market settles

Once the outcome is confirmed, contracts pay out. Yes holders get paid if it happened, No holders if it didn't. No additional margins.
