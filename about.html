The basics

What is a prediction market?

A prediction market is a platform where people trade on the outcome of real-world events, not by picking winners for fun, but by putting real money behind their informed opinion. Think of it like a stock market, except instead of trading company shares, you're trading on questions: "Will Bayern Munich win the Champions League?" "Will NASA confirm the existence of Aliens by the end of 2026?" "Will Drake's new album 'Iceman' be number one on Billboard in July?"

Every market resolves as a simple Yes or No. You buy a Yes contract if you believe the outcome is Yes, and buy a No contract if you believe the outcome is No. The price, expressed as a percentage between 0% and 100%, reflects the collective wisdom of everyone trading. That number isn't set by a bookie or an algorithm. It's the live consensus of the users.

What makes prediction markets uniquely powerful is that they aggregate information. When you have skin in the game, you think harder, research more, and factor in everything you know. In simple terms: "Put your money where your mouth is." The resulting price is often a more accurate prediction than any single expert's forecast.

How it works

Peer to peer. Three steps.

YM Predictions is a peer-to-peer platform. You're not betting against us, you're trading with other users.

01

A market opens

Example: Will Spain win Euro 2028? YM defines clear resolution rules and the official source that will determine the outcome.

02

You trade

Buy Yes if you believe it'll happen, buy No if you don't. Prices shift in real time. Sell your position before resolution to lock in profits or cut losses.

03

The market settles

Once the real-world outcome is confirmed, contracts pay out. Yes holders win if it happened; No holders win if it didn't. No house edge. No hidden margin.
