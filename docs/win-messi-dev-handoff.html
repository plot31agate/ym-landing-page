Sign-up form wiring

Fields the form submits

NameTypeRequiredNotes
sourcehiddenFixed WIN-MESSIStore on the account for attribution
emailemailYesStandard, autocomplete="email"
passwordpasswordYesminlength=8. Same rules as main platform
promo_codetextNoMax 32, uppercase-styled input. Store on account. Behaviour TBC by product
over18checkboxYes18+ confirmation

marketingcheckboxNoMarketing opt-in for future comms

Expected server behaviour

  1. Validate inputs (email, password strength, both required checkboxes present).
  2. Treat as POST /register — this is a real platform account, not a competition-only lead.
  3. Create the YM Predictions account, store source=WIN-MESSI and promo_code on the record.
  4. Send verification email (Section 04).
  5. Redirect the browser to /thank-you on success.
  6. On validation error, re-render with error messages or handle client-side. HTML5 required/minlength attributes are already set.

Critical: Fire the campaign conversion on email-verified, not on form submit. This is called out in the original brief — form-submit fires would inflate the funnel.

04

Email verification flow

Verifying the email is what actually locks the entry in and fires the campaign conversion. Called out in T&Cs Section 4.

  1. On successful registration, send a verification email with a unique tokenised link.
  2. The verification landing page must:
    • Mark the platform email as verified.
    • Mark the competition entry as valid (the draw only pulls from valid entries).
    • Fire the campaign conversion event.
    • Redirect to the Predictor or show a confirmation screen.
  3. If verification is not completed by the closing time (Sun 19 Jul), the entry is discarded.
