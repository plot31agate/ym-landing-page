Email verification

Send a verification email on registration. Clicking the link must:

  1. Mark the email as verified.
  2. Mark the competition entry as valid (only valid entries go into the draw).
  3. Fire the campaign conversion event.

Fire the campaign conversion on email verified, not on form submit.
